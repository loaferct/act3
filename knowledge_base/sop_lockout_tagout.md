# SOP-LOTO-01: Lockout / Tagout for Maintenance Work

## Scope
Mandatory for all work inside guards or on machinery where unexpected start-up or release of stored energy could cause injury. Applies to pumps, compressors, presses, conveyors and machine tools.

## Steps
1. Notify the area supervisor and affected operators.
2. Stop the machine using normal controls.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical at the MCC or local isolator, pneumatic, hydraulic (bleed accumulators to 0 bar), mechanical (lower or block raised parts such as press rams), process fluids (close and lock valves, drain).
4. Apply a personal lock and tag to every isolation point.
5. Verify zero energy: attempt start at local control, check gauges read zero, test for absence of voltage.
6. Only then begin work. Remove locks only after guards are refitted and everyone is clear.

## Hot surfaces
Bearings, compressor discharge lines and hydraulic oil can exceed 80 °C. Wear heat-resistant gloves and allow cool-down time.
