# Maintenance Manual: Oil-Injected Screw Compressor C-201 (75 kW)

## Operating limits
Normal discharge temperature 75-90 °C at full load. High temperature alarm 105 °C. Protection trip 112 °C: the controller stops the compressor to protect the oil and airend. Outlet pressure setpoint 7.5 bar, low pressure alarm 6.6 bar. Normal motor current 110-125 A at full load.

## High discharge temperature: causes
In order of likelihood: (1) fouled oil cooler - dust and oil mist on the cooler fins reduce heat transfer, temperature climbs slowly over days; (2) low oil level; (3) thermostatic valve stuck; (4) blocked oil filter; (5) high ambient temperature or blocked ventilation in the compressor room. A gradual rise of several degrees per day with rising motor current points to cooler fouling.

## Cooler cleaning procedure
1. Unload and stop the compressor, isolate per SOP-LOTO-01, wait 10 minutes for pressure to vent and verify 0 bar on the gauge.
2. Remove the cooler access panels.
3. Blow the fins clean with dry compressed air from the clean side to the dirty side, maximum 6 bar. Use degreaser for oily deposits; never use a pressure washer on the fins.
4. Inspect the cooling fan and belt.
5. Check oil level and condition; replace oil filter if differential pressure above 1.0 bar.
Typical duration: 2 hours. While C-201 is down, the standby compressor C-202 must be started to hold plant air pressure.

## Oil and filters
Oil: Roto-Inject Fluid, change every 4000 hours. Oil filter element C200-OF, on site. Air/oil separator C200-SEP, change every 8000 hours or when differential pressure exceeds 0.8 bar.

## Impact of compressor trip
Loss of plant air stops the forming presses, pneumatic clamps on the machining cells and the paint line within about 4 minutes. Always start C-202 before taking C-201 out of service.
