# Maintenance Manual: 800 t Hydraulic Press M-501

## Hydraulic system limits
Working system pressure 205-215 bar. Low pressure alarm 185 bar; below 175 bar the press control inhibits the stroke (press trip) because forming force is insufficient and parts will be out of tolerance. Oil temperature normal 40-55 °C, alarm 65 °C, trip 70 °C. Oil: ISO VG 46 HM.

## Internal leakage in the main pump
An axial piston pump with worn swash plate, pistons or valve plate leaks oil internally. Symptoms: system pressure slowly falls at the same command, oil temperature rises because leaked oil is throttled to heat, pump vibration may increase. Before condemning the pump: check the relief valve setting (it should crack at 230 bar), check for external leaks and check the case-drain flow. Case-drain flow above 6 l/min at working pressure confirms excessive internal leakage.

## Pump replacement
Exchange pump M500-HP (rebuilt unit, Rexroth A4VSO 125) held in central stores, lead time 1 day. Procedure: isolate per SOP-LOTO-01 including hydraulic energy - lower the ram onto blocks and bleed accumulators to 0 bar, verify on gauge. Replace pump, fill and bleed, run in at low pressure for 15 minutes, then calibrate pressure. Typical duration: 4 hours with a hydraulic fitter and an electrician.

## Oil temperature
Rising oil temperature also occurs with a fouled oil cooler or failed cooling-water supply (check P-101 status). Above 60 °C oil ageing doubles for every 10 °C.

## Quality impact
At system pressure below 195 bar, the first symptom is incomplete forming on deep-drawn parts. Increase first-off inspection frequency to every 50 parts when pressure is below 200 bar.
