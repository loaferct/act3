# Maintenance Manual: Centrifugal Pumps P-101 / P-102 (ISO 2858, 30 kW)

## Vibration limits
Measure bearing housing vibration as velocity RMS (mm/s) per ISO 10816-3, group 2, rigid foundation. Normal operation is below 2.8 mm/s. 2.8-7.1 mm/s: restricted, investigate at next opportunity. Above 7.1 mm/s: alarm, plan repair. Above 11.0 mm/s: protection trip, damage occurring. A steady rise over days at constant load almost always indicates rolling-element bearing degradation, misalignment or impeller imbalance.

## Bearing wear: symptoms and diagnosis
Drive-end bearing wear first shows as a slow rise in overall vibration while temperature is still normal. Bearing temperature rises later, typically in the final 20-30% of remaining life, when lubrication breaks down. Motor current rises slightly due to friction. If vibration has doubled from baseline, order a replacement bearing set (SKF 6309-2Z/C3 drive end, 6307-2Z/C3 non-drive end) and schedule the job. Do not re-grease a bearing that is already noisy and hot - it can mask the problem for hours and then seize.

## Bearing replacement procedure
1. Isolate the pump per SOP-LOTO-01: electrical isolation at MCC, close suction and discharge valves, drain casing.
2. Remove coupling guard, check and record coupling alignment before disassembly.
3. Remove the bearing housing; pull bearings with a hydraulic puller, never by hammering.
4. Inspect shaft journals for wear or fretting; replace the shaft if wear exceeds 0.02 mm.
5. Heat new bearings with an induction heater to 110 °C maximum; fit and allow to cool.
6. Reassemble, laser-align coupling to within 0.05 mm offset, refit guard.
7. Run for 30 minutes and confirm vibration below 2.8 mm/s and bearing temperature stable below 70 °C.
Typical duration: 2.5 hours with two technicians when parts are on hand.

## Cavitation and suction problems
Cavitation sounds like gravel in the pump. Indicators: falling discharge pressure, falling motor current (less hydraulic work), noisy and erratic vibration. Common causes: clogged suction strainer, partially closed suction valve, low tank level, air ingress at suction flange. First action: check the suction strainer differential pressure and clean the strainer basket (job time 45 minutes, requires isolation). Prolonged cavitation erodes the impeller and damages the mechanical seal.

## Mechanical seal
Seal type: John Crane Type 21, 45 mm. Leakage above 10 drops per minute requires replacement. Seal replacement requires pump disassembly; combine it with bearing work where possible.

## Spare parts
Bearing kit P100-BK (2 bearings + seals), lead time 1 day from central stores, 3 days from supplier. Strainer basket P100-SB, on site. Mechanical seal P100-MS, lead time 2 days.
