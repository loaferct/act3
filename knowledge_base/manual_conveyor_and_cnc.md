# Maintenance Manual: Line 4 Conveyor CV-401 and Machining Centre CNC-301

## CV-401 belt conveyor drive
Belt speed 1.6 m/s. Low speed alarm 1.45 m/s, trip 1.35 m/s (slip protection). Belt slip shows as falling belt speed at constant motor speed, rising motor temperature and gearbox vibration. Action: check belt tension at the take-up (target deflection 15 mm at mid span), clean the drive drum lagging, check for product spillage under the belt. Retensioning takes 1 hour and can be done in a short changeover stop.

## CV-401 motor
Motor winding temperature alarm 110 °C, trip 125 °C (class F insulation). Check cooling fan cover for dust.

## CNC-301 spindle
Spindle vibration normal below 2 mm/s, alarm 4.5 mm/s, trip 7.0 mm/s. Spindle temperature alarm 60 °C. A combined slow rise of spindle vibration and temperature indicates loss of bearing preload or bearing damage. Spindle repair is performed by the OEM; exchange spindle lead time 5 days, replacement takes a full shift. Early detection allows the exchange spindle to be ordered before failure, which avoids around 3 weeks of downtime waiting for an emergency rebuild.

## CNC-301 coolant
Coolant temperature above 30 °C affects dimensional accuracy. Check chiller and concentration (target 6-8%).
