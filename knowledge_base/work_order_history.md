# Maintenance Work Order History (last 18 months, selected)

## WO-2025-0412: P-101 bearing failure (unplanned)
Pump P-101 tripped on high vibration at 02:40 on night shift. Drive-end bearing outer race spalled, shaft journal fretted. No bearing kit in local stores, kit delivered next morning. Shaft replaced. Total downtime 11 hours; cooling-water loss stopped machining cells for 6 hours. Root cause: bearing life exceeded, no condition monitoring. Recommendation: monitor vibration trend, keep one bearing kit on site.

## WO-2025-0587: P-102 strainer clogged
Discharge pressure fell over three days, operators reported noise. Suction strainer found 70% blocked with swarf. Cleaned in 40 minutes during shift change. No damage.

## WO-2025-0733: C-201 high temperature trip
Compressor tripped at 112 °C in summer. Oil cooler fins heavily fouled with oily dust. Plant air lost for 25 minutes until C-202 was started manually; three presses scrapped parts in progress. Cooler cleaned, 2 hours. Recommendation: clean cooler every 3 months, alarm on temperature trend.

## WO-2025-0910: M-501 low pressure, pump exchange
Press pressure drifted down from 210 to 188 bar over a week; parts with incomplete forming found at final inspection, 1,400 parts quarantined. Case-drain flow 9 l/min. Pump exchanged in a planned weekend stop, 4 hours.

## WO-2026-0118: CV-401 belt slip
Belt speed dropped, product jammed at transfer. Take-up retensioned and drive drum cleaned, 1 hour.

## WO-2026-0302: CNC-301 spindle failure
Spindle seized during roughing. Emergency OEM rebuild took 19 days; work moved to subcontractor at extra cost of €42,000. Vibration had been rising for two weeks but was not monitored.
